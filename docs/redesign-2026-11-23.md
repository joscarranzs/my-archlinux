# Design brief — "Cabinet of Curiosities"

Date: 2026-11-23. Applies to the Quickshell/Hyprland desktop in this repository.
Companion to `design-system.md`, which stays the source of truth for the numbers;
this file records the *direction* the numbers are being pulled toward.

Purpose:        Lift the desktop from a Material-3-flavoured set of flat, translucent
                panels to a distinct, contemporary-Renaissance identity ("cabinet of
                curiosities" / wunderkammer), without touching the palette.
Audience:       The owner (single-user live system). Focused, keyboard-first, dark room.
Platform:       Quickshell 0.3.2 panels + Hyprland 0.57, Qt Quick. Colours, measures
                and motion enter QML only through the apply-theme.py pipeline.
Success:        A stranger can name the identity from a screenshot; the desktop reads
                deliberate (engraved, gilded, layered) rather than generic; no palette
                hex changes; the full test battery stays green.
Frictions:      (1) Flat 5px corners everywhere + 72% glass = "default MD3".
                (2) One accent (mint) doing every job: rule, hover, action.
                (3) No entrance motion; state-layer-only hover.
                (4) Depth comes from translucency only, not from layering.
Constraints:    Palette (warm-charcoal) is immutable. The token JSONs + apply-theme.py
                are the only way a colour or measure enters QML. Functionality and
                business logic untouched. No blur, no gradients, no shadow-everything.

## Direction

Sentence:       "Cabinet of curiosities: velvet charcoal panels, gold-leaf rules and
                inner frames, engraved serif titling, one living mint accent."
Differs from:   the current look (MD3 pastels on dark glass, flat 5px, state-layer
                hover) by four decisions: an ornament role (gold) split from the action
                accent (mint), architectural geometry, tonal layered depth, and a
                signature entrance motion.

### Candidates

| Direction | One sentence | The risk it carries | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cabinet of curiosities | velvet, gold leaf, engraved titling, one mint accent | gold reads cheap if overused | chosen |
| Dark scriptorium | parchment-on-charcoal, heavy serif, no metal | monotonous; reads as a text editor, not a desktop | rejected — loses the "object" quality |
| Neo-classical plaster | pale plaster surfaces, engraved black, no gold | needs a light palette; the palette is locked dark | rejected — violates the locked palette |

## The eight decisions (as tokens, not adjectives)

Type:        Keep the three voices — Fraunces (display/engraved), EB Garamond (body
             prose), SpaceMono (data, uppercase tracked). Scale micro..display unchanged;
             tabular numerals where figures align.
Colour:      Palette locked. Re-split the JOBS: accent.tertiary (#c3b283, the gold)
             becomes the ornament role (gilt rules, frames); accent.primary (mint)
             stays the sole action/state accent. onAccent unchanged.
Surfaces:    Layered, not flat: tonal elevation (base -> raised -> raisedHigh) plus a
             gold inner frame (the "gilt filet"). More opaque so panels read velvet,
             not glass. No blur, no gradients, no heavy shadow.
Geometry:    Architectural radius ladder none/xs/sm/md/lg/xl = 0/2/4/6/8/12, full 9999.
             Outer plaques 12, cards 6, rows 4. Sharp enough to read framed, far from
             the soft-bubble ceiling.
Rhythm:      4-based spacing scale unchanged. A `frameInset` (the mat/paspartu gap
             between plaque edge and gilt frame) arrives with the frame in phase 2.
Motion:      One signature entrance (fade + subtle lift, ~160-220 ms, decelerating)
             for panels; hover switches from a state-layer wash to a gilt ignite +
             tonal lift; press feedback. reduced-motion disables entrances. One
             entrance, one hover.
Ornament:    Budget: one gold rule per header (32x1, recoloured gilt) + one gold inner
             frame per plaque + the mint action. Out: gradients, glass blur, shadows
             everywhere, textures, literal historical ornament.
Copy voice:  Uppercase tracked labels stay ("inscription"). Errors plain and honest.
             Empty states preserved and refined.

## Out of scope

- Palette (any hex change), business logic, feature removal.
- GTK/kitty/nvim geometry (colours only, as today).
- Blur, gradients, drop shadows, textures.

## Phase plan (each phase closes green on the full battery)

1. Token layer — geometry ladder + surface depth (opacity). [done 2026-11-23]
2. Surfaces per panel — gilt frame, tonal elevation, radius step assignment.
   [done 2026-11-23: gilt edges and header rules landed; the tonal step landed
   later, with the hover that consumes it]
3. Motion — signature entrance, gilt hover, press, reduced-motion.
   [done 2026-11-23. The entrance is the fade *and* the lift: `motionEntranceOffset`
   (8) rides a `Translate` transform, because every plaque is anchored by its shell
   and a translate moves the paint without fighting `anchors.centerIn`. It lands on
   its anchor over `motionEntranceDuration` (200 ms, decelerating). Reduced motion
   zeroes the offset with the durations, so there is still no QML branch on the
   flag. `check_entrance_lift` refuses a QML file that animates the duration
   without carrying the offset, and `scripts/session/tests/panels/run-all.sh`
   asserts both halves offscreen on the eight plaques that have a component of
   their own. Hover/press/reduced-motion as below.]
4. Iconography and detail — icon steps 16/22/28, keycaps, launcher cell 112.
   [done 2026-11-23: the launcher cell is 112 and the app icon takes a new
   `iconSizeTile` (48) instead of `iconSizeXs` (16), which is what made the app
   icons read as specks. Keycaps landed next: `components/LegendRow.qml` is
   generated into every plaque that shows keys, the kanban's own KanbanLegend is
   gone (it was the only copy of the recipe), and the seven footers that spelled
   their hints out as a flat string now render caps — labels tracked and
   uppercase, the same voice the kanban already used. Two footers lost a word in
   the move: the wifi legend drops `R rescan` (four entries measure 336 px
   against 312; the key still works) and the monitor keeps `tab control` only
   because 350 px of legend fit its 372 px. The launcher's label is closed too:
   two lines with `elide` on the last one, because a 104 px column fits about
   fifteen SpaceMono characters and "mpv Media Player" (16), "Yazi File Manager"
   (17) and "kitty URL Launcher" (18) were all eliding. The cell is 136 px tall,
   so the icon plus two lines is 118 and the grid does not grow.]
5. UX and final sweep — states, flows, contrast audit, 12-panel screenshot review.
   [in progress 2026-11-23. Live sweep: every panel opened on the real compositor,
   captured with `grim`, and its QML log read. All clean except the webapp form,
   which warned about a `MouseArea` child of a `RowLayout` (undefined geometry,
   now a plain `Item` that sizes itself from the row) and the draft `FileView`
   reading a file that does not exist yet on a first run (handled by
   `onLoadFailed`, left as benign noise). Two findings from the review are fixed:
   the legend band was glued to its rule with dead space under it, and the token
   and battery plaques carried five and two interior separators that divided
   labelled blocks. Still open: the launcher's labels, the empty/error/loading
   states per panel, and the lock surface — opening it locks the live session and
   needs the owner's password, so it is checked by reading, not by sweeping.]

Status detail for phase 3, so the next session does not re-derive it:

- Hover: the object's own fill steps one rung up the tonal ladder (`hoverFill`
  `#3e353e`, or `hoverFillHigh` `#453c45` when it already rests at `hoverFill`)
  and its edge ignites `gilt`; press steps the fill down to `pressFill` `#292329`
  at 80 ms. The wash survives only as the kanban drop feedback and the monitor's
  filled APPLY button. Mint keeps selection and focus.
- Reduced motion: `reducedMotion` in `animations.json`; `apply-theme.py` emits `0`
  for every `animations.ui.*Duration` and every `animations.ui.*Offset`. Hyprland's
  own `animations.enabled` is
  untouched, so a window still flies while a plaque lands instantly.
- Entrance, measured (offscreen probe, volume plaque, 2026-11-23): the lift reads
  8.000 at the first frame, 3.512 at 40 ms, 0.681 at 100 ms, 0.014 at 170 ms and
  0.000 at rest; a grab of the same plaque puts its top gilt row at 40 px at rest
  and 48 px while held at the offset, so `mapToItem` equals the rendered edge and
  the harness can assert the paint, not just the token.
